As mentioned before, in this context a VPN is essentially your own private tunnel that is secure.
You may have heard the terms **Wireguard** or **Tailscale** when discussing about VPNs. Wireguard is the actual protocol of the VPN, whereas Tailscale uses the wireguard protocol. So why even use Tailscale? Tailscale is extremely easy to install, has other cool features, and is baked right into Unraid.

 As of Feb 2025, Tailscale Integration is quite new so there are bound to be future changes and improvements.
