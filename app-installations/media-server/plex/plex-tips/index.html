Tips

📱Plex App

I recommend using the app instead of the website. The web version often transcodes the media, and sometimes makes subtitles look weird, especially for anime.

💭 Suggested Settings

Some settings you might want to turn on or adjust include:

  • Enabling "scan my library automatically" under Library settings.
  • Enabling Direct Play/Stream to prevent transcoding, under Debug settings.
  • Setting the Video Quality to maximum under Quality settings.
  • Disable Hardware Acceleration (if available) as it may cause playback issues, under Transcoder settings.
  • For anime, adjusting the audio to Japanese and subtitles to English under Language settings (this won't interfere with English content).
  • For streaming on your mobile devices, you may want to set Home Streaming -> Recommended to play the videos at maximum quality under Quality settings.
  • You can also change the audio and subtitle settings per show. This is useful if you wanted to watch a show in a dub that wouldn't play by default for example.

🔗 PlexKodiConnect

If you use Kodi, you can also use it in conjunction with Plex, using the Kodi add on called PlexKodiConnect. This connects to your Plex library and uses its library scanning instead.

🧹 Removing Bloat

Currently on Plex when you click on a movie, you’ll see rotten tomato reviews and tidal soundtracks. This bloat can be removed. But the downside is it wouldn't auto create collections for movies anymore for some reason?? (last time I tried it at least)

To remove the reviews, click on the 3 dots next to the movie library that's on the menu to the left → Manage Library → Edit → Advanced and change the agent to The Movie Database.

To remove the Tidal Tracks, go into the Plex Settings → Online Media Sources and disable Music (I just disabled everything to get rid of it lol). After rescanning the library and restarting the app it didn’t disappear at first, but eventually it got removed.
