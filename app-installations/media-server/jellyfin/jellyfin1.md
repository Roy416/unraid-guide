## Clients 
(As of June 2026)

As I previously mentioned, finding the best client for each platform can be energy consuming. People usually end up testing a bunch of clients and go back to the web player. The issue I have  with the web player is that it doesn't consistently show subtitles when it's supposed to. New clients are constantly appearing and I didn't test every client under the sun, but here are the ones I liked.

### 🖥️ Desktop

**Plezy** - This is what I landed on for Windows/Mac OS. It's fast, has built in intro skipping, HDR tonemapping looks good, has a bunch of features you'd want, and it "just works." It is available on all platforms but they all don't have the same quality experience yet. It is vibecoded so you may find some strange UX/UI decisions, but I wouldn't have guessed otherwise. It's been 6 months since its release and new updates still come frequently. 

### 📱 Mobile

**Plezy** - UX can be clunky but it's fast, subtitles seem to work fine, and offline downloading actually works. 

**Infuse** - Often recommended as people claim it's the only client with no subtitle issues, and in my brief testing it's good. However you it is **subscription-based** ($17/yr or $99/lifetime). I couldn't even change the audio of a video without it asking me to pay. 

### 🔥 Firestick

**Wholphin** - This is a popular one. 

**Kodi + Jellyfin** - This worked well enough for me. 

## Plugins

Click on the top right, Dashboard, Plugins, and click All. These are all the plugins that come default with Jellyfin. Some plugins may require Jellyfin to be restarted after installing.  

### 🔌 Third Party Plugins
You can also add third party plugins by clicking Manage repositories and adding its repository. Here is [a list](https://github.com/awesome-jellyfin/awesome-jellyfin) of third party plugins and themes. Some notable ones are:

**Jellyfin-Enhanced** - This allows you to add a bunch of cool features. I would treat this as fun but experimental, as some features may flat out not work. 

**Intro Skipper/IntroDB** - In Jellyfin's settings intro skipping is mentioned but it doesn't do it. These plugins are the ones that actually make use of it. I'm actually not a fan of either of these. Intro-Skipper takes forever to scan through all the media. And my first impression of IntroDB was that it skipped some of the episode. Plezy has built in intro skipping and it works pretty well, making these two plugins redundant for me.  

**Themes** - By default, Jellyfin looks very boring. There are some really cool themes however, the really nice looking ones slow down the UI a lot. So it might be best to go for something light. These themes will only effect the web player and the Jellyfin Media Player.
